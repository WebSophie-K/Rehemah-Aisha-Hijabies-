About Our Boutique

Remy Aisha Hijabis is a modest fashion boutique located at Ham Buildings Grounds, Ham Block D, Shop D058, near Nakivubo Stadium in Kampala.

The boutique offers a variety of modest fashion products including hijabs, abayas, veils, dresses, skirts, camisoles and two-piece outfits.

Our aim is to provide beautiful and comfortable clothing while helping customers express their personal style through modest fashion.

Our Mission

To provide quality and fashionable modest clothing while offering friendly customer service and a pleasant shopping experience.

Our Vision

To become a trusted modest fashion boutique known for elegance, quality and customer satisfaction.
