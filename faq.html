Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to Remy Aisha Hijabis. Here are answers to some common questions about our products and shop.

1. Where is Remy Aisha Hijabis located?

Our shop is located at Ham Buildings Grounds, near Nakivubo Stadium, Ham Block D, Shop D058, Kampala, Uganda.

2. What products do you sell?

We sell abayas, hijabs, veils, veil caps, dresses, skirts, two-piece outfits, camisoles and children's abayas.

3. Do you have different colours and designs?

Yes. We offer different colours, designs and styles depending on available stock.

4. How can I place an order?

You can contact us through WhatsApp or visit our physical shop to make an enquiry.

5. How can I know the prices of products?

Contact us through WhatsApp for information about product prices and availability.

6. Do you sell children's clothing?

Yes. We have abayas designed for children.

7. Can I visit the shop physically?

Yes. Customers are welcome to visit us at our shop in Ham Buildings, Shop D058.

8. How can I contact you?

You can reach us through WhatsApp on +256 765 996 422.
